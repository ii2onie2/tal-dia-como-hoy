TikTok Auto Publisher

TikTok Auto Publisher is a web-based publishing utility for authorized creator accounts. It helps account owners schedule and publish their own video content to TikTok after explicit authorization.

How it works

The account owner connects a TikTok account using TikTok Login Kit. The application uses the granted permissions to identify the connected account, upload creator-owned videos, and publish them to the authorized profile.

The application does not publish to accounts that have not explicitly authorized access.

Permissions used

  • user.info.basic — identify the connected TikTok account.
  • video.upload — upload creator-owned video content.
  • video.publish — publish videos to the authorized creator profile.
