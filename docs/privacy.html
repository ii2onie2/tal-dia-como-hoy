Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

TikTok Auto Publisher processes only the information needed to connect authorized TikTok creator accounts and publish content requested by the account owner.

Information processed

  • Basic TikTok account identification data returned through authorized login.
  • Authorization tokens required to access the TikTok Content Posting API.
  • Creator-provided video content and captions used for publication.

Purpose

Information is used only to authenticate authorized accounts, upload videos, schedule posts, publish content, and verify publication status.

Data sharing

Personal information is not sold. Data is shared only with TikTok and infrastructure providers as necessary to operate the publishing workflow.

Retention

Authorization data is retained only as long as needed to maintain the connected account or until access is revoked. Content and logs may be retained for operational and anti-duplication purposes.

User control

Users can revoke TikTok authorization at any time through TikTok account settings or by disconnecting the integration.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the application owner through the connected developer account.
