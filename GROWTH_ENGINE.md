# Tal Día Como Hoy - Growth Engine V2

## Publication rhythm

- 10:00 — Tal Día Como Hoy
- 16:00 — El detalle que casi nadie ve
- 19:00 — ¿Sabías esto de España?
- Optional pilot format: Antes y ahora / then-and-now comparisons

## Editorial system

Rotate formats to avoid repetition:
- chronological event
- person who changed Spain
- battle/conflict
- place with history
- before/after
- three facts
- mystery / unresolved historical question, only when evidence supports the framing
- invention / science / culture
- forgotten story
- historical object, word or custom

Do not use the same format, hook pattern, main person, period or narrative structure on consecutive episodes when alternatives exist.

## Retention structure

1. 0-2 seconds: strongest factual surprise or consequence first.
2. 2-8 seconds: immediate context; no long intro or logo-only opening.
3. 8-25 seconds: two or three short factual beats with visual changes every 3-5 seconds when source media allows it.
4. Final factual turn: surface the most surprising documented detail.
5. Final 5 seconds: concise takeaway + follow CTA + natural question.
6. Keep on-screen text out of faces and other important visual subjects.
7. Prefer short date/location labels and keyword overlays instead of long paragraphs on screen.

## Duration targets

Starting with publications dated 2026-10-04:
- all three daily series target 65-80 seconds
- absolute rendered-video minimum: 61 seconds
- narration target: approximately 170-200 Spanish words
- final validation uses the rendered MP4 duration, not word count alone
Never stretch a story with filler; add sourced context, consequences, or documented details instead.

## Hook variation

Rotate among:
- surprising fact
- unanswered question
- consequence first
- "this happened here"
- before/after
- three-fact promise
- overlooked detail

Avoid repeating identical opening phrases on consecutive days.

## Audience participation

Use one natural question at the end when appropriate:
- "¿Lo sabías?"
- "¿Qué parte te sorprendió más?"
- "¿Qué historia de España quieres que contemos?"
Do not use engagement bait that misrepresents facts.

## Weekly measurement

When Metricool analytics are available, review:
- views
- average watch time / retention
- completion rate
- shares
- comments
- followers gained per post

Compare these metrics by format, topic family, duration and hook pattern. Use the measurements to refine future episodes. Do not treat views alone as evidence of quality.

## Anti-repetition

Keep a machine-readable history of published episode dates, titles, subjects, principal people, formats and hook patterns. Prefer a different subject family when a recent episode used the same one.

## Multi-platform readiness

The MP4 should remain compatible with TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels: 1080x1920, H.264/AAC, readable subtitles and no platform-specific watermark.

## Safety and accuracy

Historical and political subjects must remain factual and sourced. Do not invent quotations, motives, statistics, archival provenance or visual evidence. Preserve source and media-credit metadata.


## Video format proposals

### Tal Día Como Hoy — mini-documentary
Target from 2026-10-04: 65-80 seconds.
Structure: shock/consequence hook -> immediate historical context -> 2-3 factual turns -> strongest detail -> CTA/question.
Visual language: archival images, slow pan/zoom, date/location badge, maps or document details when available.

### ¿Sabías esto de España? — fast curiosity
Target from 2026-10-04: 65-80 seconds.
Structure: one surprising claim -> explanation -> unexpected detail -> one-line takeaway -> CTA.
Visual language: faster cuts, one main object/place, text emphasis on the surprising detail.

### El detalle que casi nadie ve — short visual discovery
Target from 2026-10-04: 65-80 seconds.
Schedule: 16:00.
Structure: ultra-direct hook -> show the detail immediately -> explain what/where -> surprising meaning/origin -> CTA/question.
Visual language: close crop, zoom/highlight, one object or architectural detail, short labels, rapid visual emphasis.
Default CTA: "Síguenos para descubrir más detalles de España que casi nadie ve."

### Antes y ahora — pilot
Target: 20-35 seconds.
Use only when there is reliable historical and present-day visual evidence for the same place/object.
Structure: old view -> what changed -> current view -> one historical fact -> CTA/question.
Do not create a third daily scheduled post automatically until performance has been reviewed.


### Este lugar fue otra cosa — weekly transformation story
Frequency: once per week.
Recommended slot: Sunday 21:30 Europe/Madrid.
Target: 25-40 seconds.
Concept: show a place people recognize today, reveal its former use, then explain the transformation with one strong documented fact.
Structure: "Este lugar fue..." hook -> old function/use -> what changed -> what it is today -> CTA/question.
Visual language: old photo or archival view first, then present-day view when reliable reusable media is available.
Default CTA: "Síguenos para descubrir los lugares de España que esconden otra historia."
