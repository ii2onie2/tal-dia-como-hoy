# Tal Día Como Hoy - Growth Engine

## Daily format

- 10:00: Tal Día Como Hoy
- 19:00: ¿Sabías esto de España?

## Editorial rules

1. The first 1-3 seconds must create a concrete curiosity gap.
2. No logo-only or generic introduction before the hook.
3. Use a clear narrative progression: hook -> context -> surprise -> consequence -> CTA.
4. Use 2-4 reusable/licensed visual assets when available; never fabricate documentary evidence.
5. Keep on-screen text short and readable on mobile.
6. End with a natural follow CTA and a question that can generate comments.
7. Avoid repeating the same subject, hook wording or visual pattern on consecutive days.
8. Political/historical controversies must be factual, sourced and neutrally framed.
9. Every episode must retain source and media-credit metadata.

## Hook bank

- "Esto ocurrió en España y casi nadie lo recuerda..."
- "Parece imposible, pero ocurrió realmente..."
- "Lo que pasó después cambió la historia de España..."
- "¿Sabías que...?"
- "Hay una razón histórica detrás de esto..."

## Weekly optimization

Review Metricool data for:
- views
- average watch time / retention when available
- completion rate when available
- shares
- comments
- followers gained per post

Use those measurements to refine future hooks, topics and durations. Do not infer performance from views alone.
