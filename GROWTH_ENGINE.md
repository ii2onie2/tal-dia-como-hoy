# Tal Día Como Hoy - Growth Engine V2

## Publication rhythm

- 10:00 — Tal Día Como Hoy
- 19:00 — ¿Sabías esto de España?

## Editorial system

Rotate formats to avoid repetition:
- chronological event
- person who changed Spain
- battle/conflict
- place with history
- before/after
- three facts
- mystery / unresolved historical question, only when evidence supports the framing
- invention / science / culture
- forgotten story
- historical object, word or custom

Do not use the same format, hook pattern, main person, period or narrative structure on consecutive episodes when alternatives exist.

## Retention structure

1. 0-3 seconds: concrete curiosity gap; no long intro or logo-only opening.
2. 3-15 seconds: immediate context.
3. Middle: progressive revelations and one or more factual turns.
4. Final 5 seconds: concise takeaway + follow CTA + natural question.
5. Keep on-screen text out of faces and other important visual subjects.

## Duration targets

Choose duration from the story:
- simple curiosity: 25-40 seconds
- standard historical story: 45-70 seconds
- complex story: 90-150 seconds
Never stretch a story merely to reach a target duration.

## Hook variation

Rotate among:
- surprising fact
- unanswered question
- consequence first
- "this happened here"
- before/after
- three-fact promise
- overlooked detail

Avoid repeating identical opening phrases on consecutive days.

## Audience participation

Use one natural question at the end when appropriate:
- "¿Lo sabías?"
- "¿Qué parte te sorprendió más?"
- "¿Qué historia de España quieres que contemos?"
Do not use engagement bait that misrepresents facts.

## Weekly measurement

When Metricool analytics are available, review:
- views
- average watch time / retention
- completion rate
- shares
- comments
- followers gained per post

Compare these metrics by format, topic family, duration and hook pattern. Use the measurements to refine future episodes. Do not treat views alone as evidence of quality.

## Anti-repetition

Keep a machine-readable history of published episode dates, titles, subjects, principal people, formats and hook patterns. Prefer a different subject family when a recent episode used the same one.

## Multi-platform readiness

The MP4 should remain compatible with TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels: 1080x1920, H.264/AAC, readable subtitles and no platform-specific watermark.

## Safety and accuracy

Historical and political subjects must remain factual and sourced. Do not invent quotations, motives, statistics, archival provenance or visual evidence. Preserve source and media-credit metadata.
